Sorry to see you go

Gmail One-Click Cleaner has been removed, and everything it kept on this device went with it. No account to close, nothing left behind.

One thing before you close this tab. Most people who uninstall hit one of four things, and three of them have a fix that takes under a minute.

The usual reasons, and what to do about them

It selected mail but never deleted any

This is the most common one and it is almost never your setup. The extension drives the real Gmail page, and Google reshuffles that page's HTML without warning, which breaks the selection and delete controls until the extension is taught the new layout.

Newer versions notice this themselves and say so instead of failing quietly. If you were on an older build, updating is the whole fix.

It cleaned far less than you expected

Four guards are on by default and they skip anything starred, marked important, unread, or already labelled. On a mailbox that stars a lot of newsletters, that alone can take a 3,000 email sweep down to a few hundred.

They live in Advanced in the popup and in Options. Turning off "skip unread" plus setting intensity to Maximum is usually the difference people are looking for.

It deleted something you wanted to keep

Nothing is destroyed. Every cleanup moves mail to Trash, where Gmail keeps it for 30 days, and every run labels what it touched before it moves anything.

Search Gmail for in:trash plus that label, select all, and move it back to the inbox. If you reinstall, the Stats page has a Restore button that does the same thing in one click for the last 20 runs.

Too many buttons

Fair. The short version is that you can ignore all of it: open the popup, press Monthly, press Run Cleaner. That is the whole product. Everything else exists for people who asked for it and stays folded away until you go looking.

If you bought Pro

Your key is still yours. It is a lifetime license tied to your purchase, not to this installation, so uninstalling did not consume it, expire it, or refund it. Install the extension on any browser, on any machine, paste the same key, and Pro is back.
