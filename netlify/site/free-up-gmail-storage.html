The catch with the free column is not the money, it is that mail comes back. If nothing changes about what arrives, you will be doing this again in a year or two. That is the real argument for the subscription, and the real argument for unsubscribing properly while you are in there.

Where the space actually is

Two things surprise most people. The first is that the 15 GB is shared with Google Drive and Google Photos, so the mailbox may not be the problem at all. Check Google's storage manager before you spend an hour in the wrong place.

The second is that it is never the count. A hundred thousand newsletters might come to a few hundred megabytes. Thirty phone videos somebody emailed you can beat that on their own. Attack size, not volume.

And deleting is only half of it

Deleted mail sits in Trash for 30 days and keeps its space the whole time. Empty Trash, and Spam, or nothing you did will show up in the storage bar. Then give it a few hours to update.

An hour, done in the right order

  1. Search larger:25M. This is usually a couple of dozen messages and a surprising number of gigabytes.
  2. Search larger:10M older_than:1y, then has:attachment larger:5M older_than:2y.
  3. For each search, tick the top-left box, then click Select all conversations that match this search. Miss that and you have only got the fifty on screen.
  4. Empty Trash. Empty Spam.
  5. Unsubscribe from the lists that filled it. This is the step that decides whether you are back here next year.

When paying Google is the right call

It is worth saying plainly, because a page selling an alternative has an obvious incentive not to. Buy the storage if you actually need the mail, if the space is going on Photos you want kept at full quality, or if an afternoon of your time is worth more than $20. Cleaning up is the right call when the space is going on things you would never miss, which for most full mailboxes is exactly what it is.

Common questions

Can I get more Gmail storage for free?

Not beyond the 15 GB that comes with the account. A second Google account gets its own 15 GB, and you can forward to it, but that is a filing system rather than extra room in the mailbox you already use.

How much is Google One?

100 GB is $1.99 a month, or $19.99 a year if you pay annually. Storage is shared across Gmail, Drive and Photos, and with up to five other people.

Will deleting emails free up space straight away?

No. It frees space once you empty Trash, and the storage figure can take a few hours to catch up after that.

Is it safe to bulk delete old mail?

Deleted mail is recoverable from Trash for 30 days, so a mistake made today is fixable today. The risk is not deletion, it is deleting something you needed and not noticing for a month, which is a good reason to work from older_than: searches rather than clearing everything.
