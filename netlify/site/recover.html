Recover your Pro key

Lost your license key? Enter the email address you paid with and a working key is issued to you again, free. It unlocks the same lifetime purchase, and any browser already running Pro carries on untouched.

Then activate it

  1. Right-click the extension icon and pick Options.
  2. Scroll to Pro License, paste the key, click Activate.

Already have Pro working in another browser? Open Options › Pro License there and use Show key to copy it across; no recovery needed.
Still stuck? Email support with your Stripe receipt.
